# 14. Operational Architecture & Observability

---

# Purpose

The Operational Architecture defines how the platform is monitored, maintained and operated in production.

The purpose is to ensure that platform health, connector execution, knowledge processing, AI services and business intelligence remain observable, measurable and explainable throughout the entire lifecycle.

Operational capabilities are considered first-class architectural components rather than implementation details.

---

# Operational Objectives

The platform shall provide:

- complete operational visibility
- proactive monitoring
- health assessment
- performance metrics
- diagnostics
- auditing
- operational analytics
- automated alerting
- incident investigation
- continuous improvement

# Architectural Decisions

Operations are treated as a platform capability.

Health is measured independently for each platform.

AI operations are monitored through the AI Gateway.

Business metrics complement technical metrics.

Operational visibility extends from infrastructure to recommendations.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **If a capability cannot be observed, measured and explained, it cannot be trusted.**

Operational Architecture provides continuous visibility into every layer of the platform while preserving the separation between infrastructure, knowledge and business intelligence.
