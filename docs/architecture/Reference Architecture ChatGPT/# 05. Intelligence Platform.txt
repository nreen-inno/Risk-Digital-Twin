# 05. Intelligence Platform

---

# Purpose

The Intelligence Platform transforms reusable organisational knowledge into business intelligence.

Unlike the Knowledge Platform, which extracts facts from information, the Intelligence Platform evaluates what those facts mean for the organisation.

The primary outputs are:

- Risk Signals
- Recommendations
- Business Insights
- Future AI Agent actions

The Intelligence Platform is independent of information collection and knowledge extraction.

---

# Position in Overall Architecture

```
Information Sources
        │
        ▼
Connector Platform
        │
        ▼
Knowledge Platform
        │
        ▼
Intelligence Platform
        │
        ▼
Presentation Platform
```

Knowledge answers:

> What happened?

The Intelligence Platform answers:

> Why does it matter?

---

# Objectives

The Intelligence Platform shall:

- assess business impact
- generate Risk Signals
- classify enterprise risks
- estimate severity
- estimate probability
- estimate confidence
- generate recommendations
- support explainable reasoning
- support future autonomous AI agents

---

# Intelligence Pipeline

```
Knowledge
      │
      ▼
Monitoring Objectives
      │
      ▼
Risk Assessment
      │
      ▼
Risk Signals
      │
      ▼
Recommendations
      │
      ▼
Presentation
```

---

# Monitoring Objectives

Monitoring Objectives represent business intent.

Examples include:

- Supplier Stability
- Geopolitical Risk
- Shipyard Logistics
- Energy Market
- Labour Availability
- Environmental Compliance

Monitoring Objectives determine:

- which Knowledge is relevant
- which enterprise risks should be evaluated
- recommendation priorities

Knowledge remains independent from Monitoring Objectives.

---

# Risk Assessment

Purpose

Evaluate the business impact of reusable Knowledge.

Risk Assessment combines:

- Knowledge
- Monitoring Objective
- Enterprise Risk Taxonomy
- Business Rules
- AI reasoning

The output is one or more Risk Signals.

---

# Risk Signals

A Risk Signal represents a business-relevant interpretation of Knowledge.

A Risk Signal always references:

- Monitoring Objective
- Knowledge
- Enterprise Risk Category

Typical fields:

- severity
- probability
- confidence
- business impact
- explanation
- evidence
- timestamp

One Knowledge object may generate multiple Risk Signals.

Example:

```
Knowledge

↓

Port strike

↓

Logistics Risk

↓

Operational Risk

↓

Supply Chain Risk
```

---

# Recommendations

Recommendations represent suggested actions.

Examples:

- Increase monitoring frequency
- Contact supplier
- Review delivery schedule
- Escalate to project manager
- Ignore (low significance)

Recommendations should always explain:

- why
- based on which evidence
- confidence

---

# Enterprise Risk Taxonomy

Risk Signals should map to a common enterprise taxonomy.

Example categories:

- Strategic
- Operational
- Financial
- Supply Chain
- Cyber
- Environmental
- Regulatory
- Political
- Reputation

The taxonomy remains independent from Knowledge.

---

# Business Rules

Not every decision should require AI.

Business Rules may include:

- deterministic thresholds
- organisational policies
- escalation matrices
- notification rules
- confidence thresholds

Business Rules and AI should complement each other.

---

# Explainability

Every Risk Signal must be explainable.

The reasoning chain is:

```
Recommendation
        │
        ▼
Risk Signal
        │
        ▼
Knowledge
        │
        ▼
Raw Record
        │
        ▼
Information Source
```

Users must always be able to inspect the complete evidence chain.

---

# Confidence

Confidence should be calculated independently for each stage.

Examples:

Knowledge confidence

↓

Risk Assessment confidence

↓

Recommendation confidence

This prevents uncertainty from being hidden.

---

# Future AI Agents

The Intelligence Platform is designed to support autonomous agents.

Possible future agents:

- Supplier Agent
- Geopolitical Agent
- Environmental Agent
- Weather Agent
- Logistics Agent
- Cyber Agent

Agents consume Knowledge rather than raw documents.

This enables multiple specialised agents to reason over the same organisational memory.

---

# Cross-Objective Intelligence

One Knowledge object may affect multiple Monitoring Objectives.

Example:

```
Knowledge

↓

New EU sanctions

↓

Supplier Stability

↓

Operational Risk

↓

Financial Risk

↓

Compliance

↓

Project Delivery
```

Knowledge is processed once.

Business intelligence is generated many times.

---

# Architectural Decisions

Business reasoning belongs to the Intelligence Platform.

Knowledge remains objective.

Risk Signals are objective-specific.

Recommendations are contextual.

Business Rules complement AI rather than replacing it.

All outputs remain traceable to original evidence.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **Knowledge represents facts. Intelligence represents business meaning.**

The Intelligence Platform transforms organisational knowledge into explainable, evidence-based decision support while remaining independent from information collection and knowledge extraction.
