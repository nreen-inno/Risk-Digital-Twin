# 19. Reference Business Scenarios

---

# Purpose

This chapter demonstrates how the AI Knowledge & Risk Intelligence Platform operates in realistic business scenarios.

Unlike functional use cases, these scenarios validate the interaction between architectural platforms, services, canonical data objects and the AI Gateway.

Each scenario follows complete end-to-end processing from user interaction through knowledge generation and business intelligence.

The scenarios serve as reference implementations for developers, solution architects and AI coding assistants.

---

# Scenario Structure

Every scenario documents:

- Business Goal
- Participating Platforms
- Primary Actors
- Business Flow
- AI Gateway Interaction
- Domain Objects
- Platform Events
- Result

- AI is accessed only through the AI Gateway
- knowledge is reusable
- recommendations remain traceable
- connectors remain technology independent
- business logic remains outside the Presentation Platform

# Guiding Principle

> **Every business scenario follows the same architectural pipeline, ensuring consistency, traceability and reuse across the entire platform.**
I think we've reached the final conceptual breakthrough

While writing this chapter, I realized the Context Builder is actually the heart of the entire platform, not the LLM.

The LLM is interchangeable.

The Context Builder is not.

It becomes the component that understands what information is required for a business decision.

I would actually expand it into its own subsystem:

Context Builder
│
├── Knowledge Retriever
├── Monitoring Objective Resolver
├── Risk Taxonomy Resolver
├── Semantic Search
├── Conversation Memory
├── Evidence Collector
├── Prompt Variable Resolver
├── Context Optimizer
└── Context Validator

This is where your platform becomes fundamentally different from a generic chatbot. Instead of simply sending user prompts to an LLM, it constructs a rich, governed, business-specific context package that enables consistent and explainable AI reasoning. I think this is one of the strongest architectural ideas we've developed across the entire document.
