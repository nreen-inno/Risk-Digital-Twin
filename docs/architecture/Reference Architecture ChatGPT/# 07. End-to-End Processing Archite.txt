# 07. End-to-End Processing Architecture

---

# Purpose

This chapter describes how information flows through the platform from its original source to actionable business intelligence.

Rather than focusing on individual components, this chapter explains how the complete platform operates as an integrated system.

It provides the conceptual processing pipeline that connects all architectural platforms.

---

# End-to-End Processing Pipeline

```
Business Platform
        │
        ▼
Acquisition Platform
        │
        ▼
Knowledge Platform
        │
        ▼
Intelligence Platform
        │
        ▼
Presentation Platform
```

Each platform performs a clearly defined transformation.

---

# Step 1 — Business Configuration

The process begins with business users.

Business users define:

- Monitoring Objectives
- Information Sources
- Monitoring Profiles

These objects describe **what** the organisation wants to monitor.

No technical implementation exists yet.

---

# Step 2 — AI Connector Design

For every Information Source the platform generates a Connector Proposal.

The proposal contains:

- recommended connection method
- technical assumptions
- business scope
- monitoring configuration
- validation plan

The user may:

- accept
- modify
- regenerate

Once accepted, the proposal becomes a Connector Specification.

---

# Step 3 — Connector Generation

The platform automatically generates a Connector Definition.

The Connector Definition contains executable runtime information.

Examples include:

- adapter type
- polling schedule
- parser
- authentication
- field mapping

The Definition becomes part of the Acquisition Platform.

---

# Step 4 — Connector Validation

Before activation the platform performs automated testing.

Validation includes:

- endpoint availability
- authentication
- parser validation
- schema discovery
- sample data collection
- duplicate detection

Successful validation produces a Connector Test Result.

---

# Step 5 — Information Acquisition

Active connectors execute according to schedule.

Runtime responsibilities include:

- connecting
- downloading
- parsing
- mapping

Every collected item becomes a Raw Record.

The runtime does not perform business interpretation.

---

# Step 6 — Knowledge Processing

Raw Records are submitted to the Knowledge Platform.

Knowledge Processing performs:

- language detection
- translation
- metadata normalisation
- entity recognition
- event extraction
- relationship extraction
- topic classification

The output is a reusable Knowledge object.

Knowledge Processing may combine:

- AI
- deterministic rules
- NLP services
- future processing technologies

The platform stores Knowledge rather than AI-specific outputs.

---

# Step 7 — Risk Assessment

Knowledge objects are evaluated against active Monitoring Objectives.

The Risk Assessment Service combines:

- Knowledge
- Monitoring Objectives
- Monitoring Profiles
- Enterprise Risk Taxonomy
- Business Rules

The output is one or more Risk Signals.

One Knowledge object may generate multiple Risk Signals.

---

# Step 8 — Recommendation Generation

Recommendations are generated from Risk Signals.

Examples include:

- notify stakeholders
- increase monitoring frequency
- contact supplier
- investigate further

Recommendations always reference:

- Risk Signal
- Knowledge
- Evidence

---

# Step 9 — Presentation

The Presentation Platform displays:

- dashboards
- alerts
- trends
- evidence
- recommendations

The Presentation Platform never communicates directly with connectors.

It consumes Risk Signals and Recommendations only.

---

# Processing Responsibilities

| Platform | Input | Output |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Business Platform | User decisions | Configuration |
| Acquisition Platform | Information Sources | Raw Records |
| Knowledge Platform | Raw Records | Knowledge |
| Intelligence Platform | Knowledge | Risk Signals & Recommendations |
| Presentation Platform | Risk Signals | Decision Support |

---

# Information Flow

```
Information Source

↓

Connector

↓

Raw Record

↓

Knowledge

↓

Risk Signal

↓

Recommendation

↓

Dashboard
```

Each stage represents a transformation.

No stage should bypass another.

---

# Traceability

Every Recommendation is fully traceable.

```
Recommendation

↓

Risk Signal

↓

Knowledge

↓

Raw Record

↓

Connector Execution

↓

Connector Definition

↓

Information Source
```

This provides complete explainability.

---

# Processing Independence

Each platform is independent.

For example:

The Knowledge Platform can evolve without modifying connectors.

The Intelligence Platform can adopt new AI models without modifying the Knowledge Platform.

Presentation can evolve independently from business logic.

This separation reduces coupling and simplifies long-term evolution.

---

# Future Evolution

The architecture supports future capabilities including:

- Knowledge Graph
- Semantic Search
- Autonomous AI Agents
- Predictive Risk Analysis
- Cross-source reasoning
- Human feedback loops

These capabilities extend existing platforms rather than replacing them.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **Collect information once. Build knowledge once. Reuse knowledge many times. Generate intelligence where it creates business value.**

Business Intent
        ↓
Information
        ↓
Knowledge
        ↓
Intelligence
        ↓
Decision Support
