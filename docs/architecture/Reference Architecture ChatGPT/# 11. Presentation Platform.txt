# 11. Presentation Platform

---

# Purpose

The Presentation Platform provides human interaction with the AI Knowledge & Risk Intelligence Platform.

Unlike traditional user interfaces, the Presentation Platform does not contain business logic.

Its purpose is to present business capabilities, support decision making and enable collaboration between users and Artificial Intelligence.

The Presentation Platform consumes services exposed by the backend and presents them in a form suitable for different user roles.

---

# Design Principles

The Presentation Platform follows several fundamental principles.

## Workspace-Oriented

The user works inside dedicated workspaces.

Each workspace represents a business activity rather than a database object.

Examples include:

- Source Workspace
- Monitoring Workspace
- Connector Workspace
- Knowledge Workspace
- Risk Workspace
- Dashboard Workspace

---

## AI-First User Experience

Artificial Intelligence is considered a permanent participant in every workspace.

The user should never need to leave a workspace to interact with AI.

AI should:

- explain
- recommend
- refine
- validate
- generate
- summarise

The interface should minimise technical complexity.

---

## Progressive Disclosure

Risk Managers should see business concepts first.

Technical details should only appear when required.

Example:

```
Connector

↓

Recommended RSS connector

↓

Advanced

↓

Authentication

↓

Parser

↓

Headers
```

The majority of users should never need to open the advanced view.

---

# Presentation Architecture

```
Navigation

↓

Workspace

↓

Business Components

↓

Platform APIs
```

Business Components never communicate directly with databases.

---

# Main Workspaces

## Dashboard Workspace

Purpose

Provide an overview of organisational risk.

Typical widgets:

- Active Monitoring Objectives
- Information Sources
- Active Connectors
- Recent Risk Signals
- Recommendations
- System Health

---

## Source Workspace

Purpose

Manage Information Sources.

Capabilities:

- create source
- AI onboarding
- connector proposal
- connector refinement
- connector approval
- connector testing
- runtime monitoring

The Source Workspace is the primary entry point for new connectors.

---

## Monitoring Workspace

Purpose

Configure business monitoring.

Contains:

- Monitoring Objectives
- Monitoring Profiles
- Enterprise Risk Mapping

---

## Knowledge Workspace

Purpose

Explore organisational knowledge.

Capabilities:

- search knowledge
- inspect entities
- inspect relationships
- browse events
- evidence tracing
- semantic search (future)

---

## Risk Workspace

Purpose

Analyse generated Risk Signals.

Capabilities:

- filter
- prioritise
- acknowledge
- investigate
- inspect evidence
- generate reports

---

## Administration Workspace

Purpose

Manage platform configuration.

Capabilities:

- users
- permissions
- connector health
- audit logs
- system configuration

---

# User Roles

The platform supports multiple user roles.

Examples:

Risk Manager

Project Manager

Researcher

System Administrator

Connector Developer

AI Engineer

Different roles may use different workspaces while sharing the same backend services.

---

# AI Conversations

AI interactions are embedded inside workspaces.

Example:

```
Source Workspace

↓

AI Conversation

↓

Connector Proposal

↓

Refinement

↓

Approval
```

The user should perceive AI as a collaborative assistant rather than a separate application.

---

# Workspace Navigation

Navigation is organised around business activities rather than technical objects.

Suggested navigation:

```
Dashboard

Monitoring

Information Sources

Knowledge

Risk Signals

Reports

Administration
```

Future workspaces may be added without redesigning the navigation structure.

---

# Component Design

Presentation components are divided into three categories.

## Business Components

Examples:

- Monitoring Objective Editor
- Source Workspace
- Knowledge Explorer
- Risk Signal Viewer

---

## Shared Components

Examples:

- Search
- Tables
- Filters
- Dialogs
- Notifications

---

## AI Components

Examples:

- AI Conversation
- AI Recommendation Panel
- AI Explanation
- AI Validation
- AI Refinement

AI Components communicate with business APIs rather than directly with AI models.

---

# State Management

Presentation state is divided into:

Application State

↓

Workspace State

↓

Component State

Business data is retrieved from backend APIs.

The frontend does not duplicate business logic.

---

# Presentation Responsibilities

The Presentation Platform is responsible for:

- visualisation
- navigation
- interaction
- collaboration
- explanation

The Presentation Platform is **not** responsible for:

- business rules
- connector execution
- knowledge processing
- risk assessment

---

# Future Evolution

The architecture supports additional presentation channels including:

- Mobile applications
- Microsoft Teams
- Copilot extensions
- Voice assistants
- Command-line tools
- AR/VR interfaces

All channels consume the same business APIs.

---

# Architectural Decisions

Presentation is organised around business workspaces.

AI is integrated into every workspace.

Business logic remains in backend services.

The Presentation Platform communicates only through platform APIs.

Presentation technology may evolve independently of business capabilities.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **Users work in business workspaces. AI works alongside them. The Presentation Platform makes complex intelligence simple, explainable and actionable.**
