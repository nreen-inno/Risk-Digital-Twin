# 04. Knowledge Platform

---

# Purpose

The Knowledge Platform is responsible for transforming heterogeneous information collected from external and internal sources into reusable organisational knowledge.

Unlike traditional monitoring systems, the Risk Digital Twin does not generate business conclusions directly from collected documents.

Instead, the platform first constructs a reusable knowledge representation that becomes the foundation for business intelligence, risk assessment and future AI reasoning.

The Knowledge Platform therefore represents the organisational memory of the Risk Digital Twin.

---

# Why a Knowledge Platform?

Traditional monitoring systems repeatedly analyse the same document every time a different business question is asked.

For example:

```
Article

↓

"What does this mean for logistics?"

↓

AI
```

Later

```
Same Article

↓

"What does this mean for suppliers?"

↓

AI
```

Later

```
Same Article

↓

"What does this mean for geopolitical risk?"

↓

AI
```

The same article may therefore be processed many times.

The Risk Digital Twin follows a different approach.

```
Article

↓

Knowledge

↓

Logistics

Supplier

Political

Financial

Environmental
```

Knowledge is extracted once and reused many times.

---

# Objectives

The Knowledge Platform shall:

- extract reusable knowledge
- normalize information
- remove duplication
- identify entities
- detect relationships
- detect events
- classify topics
- preserve evidence
- maintain confidence
- support semantic search
- support future Knowledge Graph

---

# Knowledge Processing

Knowledge is created by the Knowledge Processing Service.

Knowledge Processing may combine multiple technologies.

Examples include:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Named Entity Recognition
- Rule-based extraction
- Natural Language Processing
- Translation
- Metadata extraction
- Language detection
- Relationship extraction
- Future AI agents

The implementation technology is not part of the domain model.

---

# Knowledge Object

The Knowledge object is the primary output of the processing pipeline.

Unlike AI-specific outputs, Knowledge represents reusable organisational information.

Knowledge may contain:

- timestamp

# Knowledge Repository

Knowledge objects are stored independently from Raw Records.

The repository becomes the organisational memory of the platform.

Future capabilities include:

- semantic search
- similarity search
- historical reasoning
- cross-source correlation
- trend detection
- knowledge graph

# Knowledge Quality

Every Knowledge object contains quality metadata.

Examples:

- confidence
- processing model
- extraction timestamp
- language
- completeness
- validation status

Knowledge quality supports explainability and auditing.

---

# Relationship with Monitoring Objectives

Knowledge is independent from Monitoring Objectives.

One Knowledge object may support many Monitoring Objectives.

```
Knowledge

↓

Objective A

↓

Risk Signal A


Knowledge

↓

Objective B

↓

Risk Signal B
```

This allows the platform to avoid repeated AI processing.

---

# Relationship with Risk Assessment

Knowledge itself contains no business interpretation.

Business interpretation is performed later.

```
Knowledge

↓

Risk Assessment

↓

Risk Signal
```

This separation allows business rules to evolve independently of information extraction.

---

# Future Knowledge Graph

The Knowledge Platform is intentionally designed to evolve into a Knowledge Graph.

Initially, knowledge may be stored as independent objects.

Later these objects may be connected through graph relationships.

Examples:

```
Supplier

↓

located in

↓

Finland
```

```
Company

↓

owns

↓

Shipyard
```

```
Strike

↓

affects

↓

Supplier
```

This transition should not require changes to connector implementations.

---

# Architectural Decisions

Knowledge is treated as a first-class domain object.

The platform stores knowledge rather than AI outputs.

Knowledge is reusable.

Knowledge is independent of Monitoring Objectives.

Knowledge Processing is a service.

Knowledge is persistent.

Business interpretation belongs to the Risk Assessment Platform.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **Extract knowledge once. Reuse knowledge everywhere.**

The Knowledge Platform represents the long-term organisational memory of the Risk Digital Twin and forms the foundation for explainable AI, semantic search, future Knowledge Graph capabilities and reusable business intelligence.
