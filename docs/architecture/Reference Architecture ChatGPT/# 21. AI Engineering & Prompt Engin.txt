# 21. AI Engineering & Prompt Engineering Architecture

---

# Purpose

This chapter defines how Artificial Intelligence capabilities are designed, developed, evaluated, deployed and continuously improved within the AI Knowledge & Risk Intelligence Platform.

Artificial Intelligence is treated as an engineering discipline rather than an external service.

The objective is to ensure that AI capabilities remain reliable, explainable, reusable, measurable and continuously improvable throughout the lifecycle of the platform.

---

# AI Engineering Principles

The platform follows several fundamental principles.

## AI is a Platform Capability

Artificial Intelligence is not embedded inside individual business services.

Instead, every AI interaction is managed through the AI Gateway.

Business services ask business questions.

The AI Gateway determines:

- context
- prompts
- models
- providers
- governance
- caching

---

## Prompts are Software Assets

Prompt templates are considered first-class software artefacts.

Every prompt has:

- identifier
- owner
- version
- status
- purpose
- documentation

Prompts follow the same lifecycle as application code.

---

## Context is More Important than the Model

Large Language Models provide reasoning capability.

The Context Builder provides business intelligence.

The quality of AI responses depends primarily on:

- relevant context
- structured knowledge
- business objectives
- enterprise taxonomy
- evidence

rather than on the specific AI model.

---

## AI must be Explainable

Every AI-generated output must reference:

- prompt version
- context package
- model
- evidence
- confidence

No recommendation should be generated without traceability.

The Prompt Registry manages all prompt templates.

Typical metadata includes:

- Prompt ID
- Name
- Description
- Version
- Owner
- Business Capability
- AI Task
- Status
- Created Date
- Last Modified

Prompt templates should never be embedded directly inside application code.

# Context Engineering

The Context Builder constructs the information package required for AI reasoning.

Typical inputs include:

- Knowledge Objects
- Monitoring Objectives
- Monitoring Profiles
- Enterprise Risk Taxonomy
- Historical Knowledge
- Previous Risk Signals
- User Role
- Platform Configuration
- Semantic Search Results

The Context Builder determines what the AI should know before reasoning begins.

# Architectural Decisions

Artificial Intelligence is engineered rather than embedded.

Prompts are version-controlled assets.

Context engineering is separated from prompt engineering.

AI evaluation is continuous.

Human feedback drives prompt improvement.

AI remains explainable and governed.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **Successful enterprise AI depends more on high-quality context, governance and continuous engineering than on the choice of language model.**

The AI Engineering Architecture ensures that Artificial Intelligence evolves as a managed engineering capability that remains transparent, measurable and aligned with business objectives.
