Risk case · architecture-aligned

Reflecting risk sources & risk factors in the case

Per the backend/AI reference architecture, a case is a Risk Signal: generated by AI Enrichment of RawRecords that connectors collect from Information Sources — and every risk must stay traceable to its evidence and source. These proposals surface that provenance directly on the case (using the Supplier Insolvency case as the example), so a risk manager sees not just what the risk is, but where it comes from and how confident it is.

Recommendation — combine A + B + D

They layer cleanly: D adds the Risk-Signal provenance strip to the header (always visible), A turns each risk factor into an evidenced, source-traced row (the “why”, made defensible), and B adds the “Risk sources & evidence” panel (the “where from”, auditable). C is the optional deep-dive for anyone who wants the full derivation — good behind an “Inspect evidence” click rather than always on screen.

This keeps the case readable for an executive while making it fully traceable underneath — exactly the architecture's intent: dashboards consume Risk Signals, but every signal stays connected to its evidence and sources. None of it needs real backend data yet; when Risk Signals, RawRecords and AIEnrichments exist, these blocks bind straight to them (source name, observation, confidence, generatedAt).
