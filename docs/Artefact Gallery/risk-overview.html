Why this as the starting page

It answers the executive's first question — “where is my risk right now?” — before any configuration. Each tile is a live risk signal (score, 6-month trend, top drivers) and a doorway: Open objective → drills straight into the workspace we built (/monitoring-objectives/:id), where the same objective's sources, setup and AI advisor live. One object — the monitoring objective — carries the executive headline and the management detail.

The tiles reuse your “Signal Room” unicorn-card concept, elevated with a composite risk gauge and portfolio sorting. The top strip is a lightweight first slice of the fuller Risk Room (KPI bar, heatmap, time machine) that can grow above this grid later.
