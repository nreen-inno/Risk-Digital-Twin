Interim risk scoring · test version

Risk model playground

Each objective's score is computed live from its drivers using likelihood × impact on a 1–5 scale, normalised to 0–100. Change any driver's likelihood, impact or weight — or switch the aggregation method — and the gauges, levels and contributions update instantly. This mirrors lib/riskModel.js + data/riskAssumptions.js exactly.

Aggregation
Critical ≥75 High 60–74 Elevated 45–59 Stable <45

The logic, in one line each

driver score = likelihood(1–5) × impact(1–5) ÷ 25 × 100
objective score = maxBlend: ½ · worst driver + ½ · weighted average  |  weightedAverage: mean of weighted drivers  |  softOr: 1 − Π(1 − score)^weight

Everything is deterministic and explainable — no hidden weights, no randomness. The numbers seeded here are analyst assumptions (the “test version”); when the backend returns real driver data or a finished score, the same assessObjective() consumes it unchanged. maxBlend is the default because weightedAverage dilutes a single severe driver and softOr saturates near 100.
